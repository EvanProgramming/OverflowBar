# Using OverflowBar v1.5

Open OverflowBar from Applications. The first launch walks you through permissions and icon selection. Later launches open the Menu Bar page.

1. Enable Accessibility so OverflowBar can hide selected icons and open their menus.
2. Screen Recording is optional. It shows live icon previews; without it, OverflowBar uses available app icons.
3. In **Choose Icons**, select the icons you want in the second row.
4. Choose **Hide Icons & Finish**. OverflowBar shows the current status and explains any issue.
5. Click **Open OverflowBar** in the menu bar, then select an icon to open its original menu or popover.

Turn off **Hide selected icons** to show them in the menu bar. During an operation, choose **Cancel** to stop it. **Reset Icon Selection** clears the saved selection and shows all icons. Quitting shows icons in the menu bar and keeps your saved choices.

## Permissions and limited states

Without Accessibility, icons stay in the menu bar. Without Screen Recording, live previews may be unavailable and OverflowBar uses available app icons. Items that can't be identified safely stay visible.

Ad-hoc-signed updates can invalidate old permission grants. Remove only OverflowBar's stale entry from the affected Privacy & Security list, add the current app from Applications and enable it again. Do not change unrelated apps' permissions.

## Recovery

If an icon can't be identified or moved, it stays available in the menu bar and the Menu Bar page explains what to try next.

On macOS 27 and later, OverflowBar leaves icons in the menu bar. Arranging or opening an icon can briefly move and restore the pointer.
